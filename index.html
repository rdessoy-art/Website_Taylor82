About the rider

Mark Taylor is the 2025 and 2026 British Champion in the highly competitive Lansdowne Classic 500cc championship. Having successfully reached the top of UK Classic racing, he is ready to step up to the British BMW F900R Championship.

A ten-time championship winner, five of these being in single make championships, Mark feels his ability and experience will suit the BMW F900R Championship.

Not only a successful racer, Mark has also put back into the sport as an ACU instructor. He is therefore already well known in the motorcycle community.
